Design and Experimental Validation of AntBot: An Accessible Hexapod Platform for Surveillance

Kaushal S et al. — Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham

Problem Statement

Commercial hexapods ($5K-$50K) require proprietary software, limiting university research. Furthermore, disaster zones lack GPS, demanding autonomous navigation without external positioning.

Architecture & Approach

Engineered a 21-DOF hexapod with custom inverse kinematics (MATLAB validated). Integrated OpenCV for GPS-denied navigation. Built with commodity hardware for ~5% of commercial cost.

Validation Results

  • Stable autonomous locomotion in unstructured terrain
  • Real-world execution of MATLAB-validated tripod gait
  • Persistent surveillance via onboard computer vision
